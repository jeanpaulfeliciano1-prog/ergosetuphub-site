FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy everything at once?

No. If you can only fix one thing, start with monitor height — it's the cheapest, fastest change and affects your neck and eyes the most.

Is an expensive chair actually worth it?

If you sit for 6+ hours a day, yes — the adjustability and lumbar support on a chair like the Aeron or Series 1 pay for themselves in reduced back pain over a year or two. If budget is tight, a well-adjusted mid-range chair plus a lumbar cushion is a reasonable middle ground.

Standing desk or sitting desk?

Neither, exclusively — the research consistently points to alternating between the two through the day rather than standing all day, which brings its own fatigue.
