About ErgoSetupHub

ErgoSetupHub is a small, independent site focused on one thing: helping people set up a home office that doesn't leave them sore at the end of the day. We cover chairs, desks, monitor arms, keyboards, mice, and lighting — the handful of things that make the biggest difference to how a workspace actually feels to use.

We're not a large publication with a testing lab. We research products carefully, favor well-reviewed items with a clear ergonomic rationale, and link out to Amazon so you can see current pricing, sizes, and colors for yourself.

How the site makes money

ErgoSetupHub is a participant in the Amazon Associates program. When you click through to Amazon from one of our links and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. See our full disclosure for details.
